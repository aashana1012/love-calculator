Frequently asked questions

Is the love calculator accurate?

LoveMeter is made for fun. Real relationships are built on communication, respect and shared values, not on name math. Enjoy the result and don't take it too seriously.

Why do I get the same result every time?

The score is generated deterministically from the two names, so the same pair always produces the same percentage no matter how many times you try.

Do you store the names I enter?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
